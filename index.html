Dr. Amarpal "Paul" Khanna

Industry Expert

Microwave Components & Integrated Sub-Assemblies

President of Apionics, Dr. Amarpal "Paul" Khanna, collaborates with a team of expert consultants to deliver microwave component solutions.

Dr. Khanna is an IEEE Life Fellow who is well regarded within the RF and microwave industry. He has held leadership positions at Avantek, Hewlett Packard, Agilent Technologies, Phasematrix, and National Instruments (NI), and in 2013 he was inducted into the list of honorary Microwave Legends by Microwaves & RF.

Apionics designs and develops voltage-controlled oscillators (VCOs), fixed-tuned oscillators (FTOs), digitally-tuned oscillators (DTOs), high-speed clocks, and frequency conversion components. Apionics also designs custom assemblies for a wide variety of microwave, millimeter wave and RFoverFiber applications. Microwave functions include amplifiers, oscillators, up/downconverters, frequency multipliers and dividers, SPMT switches, filters, variable attenuators, diplexers and limiters.
